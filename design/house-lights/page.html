VoxCoach design system · v1

House Lights

VoxCoach works in two lighting states. With the house lights up, you plan, read and review on paper, laid out like a score. When you start to sing, the lights go down: the stage goes dark and the brightest thing left is your voice.

House lights up

Home, Song Lab, results, progress, profile. Ruled paper, ink type, full detail.

House lights down

Singing, rehearsal, performance, games. A dark stage, only what helps you sing.

Dark changes the paper to dusk. The stage stays dark in every theme.

Press Start rehearsal, then pause. That's the change every singing screen makes.

Eight screens, eight personalities, one product

Each screen answers the brief's test under its name: what you notice first, second, and what waits until you ask. Every feature shown exists in the app today.

Example data: names, scores and dates are illustrations

Five rules

  1. Your voice is the brightest thing on screen.

    Ultramarine belongs to the singer: the live pitch line, your range, your takes, and the button that starts you singing. Nothing else uses it, so wherever it appears, it's you.

  2. Lights down to sing, lights up to learn.

    Starting the mic dims the house. While you sing you see the target, your line and how far off you are. When you stop, the lights come up on the full report and one clear next step.

  3. Paper is ruled, not boxed.

    Information sits on hairline rules, like staff lines. A screen gets at most one raised block: the thing to do now. That replaces the 26 card styles the app uses today.

  4. The coach has its own voice.

    When VoxCoach speaks to you, it uses an italic serif, in one or two specific sentences about notes, bars and seconds. Measurements use the sans. Advice and data never look alike.

  5. Measured, or not shown.

    Every number comes from something the app really measured, as it does today. How it was measured is explained once, in a folded note under the result, instead of in every paragraph.

How this is measured

How this is measured

Each note counts when the middle of your pitch readings is within ±25 cents of the target. Expression isn't scored: there's no reliable way to measure it.

The honesty rule, said once and folded away. Replaces the "real", "honest" and PRD text across the app.

Navigation

The five tabs stay. Inside them, beginners see one next step and everyone can still reach all 46 screens.

  • Tabs: Home, Coach, Songs, World, You. "Train" becomes "Coach". The active tab shows a short playhead line in your colour.
  • Header: the wordmark and your initial. The streak and level move into Home, so signed-out visitors no longer see a zero streak. Signed out, the header shows "Sign in".
  • Coach tab: today's focus first, from the Skill Profile the app already keeps. Then the 23 tools as seven named groups of rows, each with your last result. Nothing is removed.
  • Songs tab: Choir World first, with "continue your rehearsal". Then Karaoke, Studio, AI Duet, Performance Director and Stay in Key.
  • Choir World: one song page with five views: Rehearse, Song Lab, Trainers, Your Choir, Passport. These are the existing sections regrouped, with every element ID kept.
  • No dead ends: every result screen ends with one "Next" action chosen from what the app already knows, plus "Sing it again".
  • Overlaps grouped, not cut: the five progress screens sit under You as one place. Smart Warmup and the new pre-session warm-up become one warm-up.

Decisions I've made

You gave me creative authority, so these are made. Each one is easy to reverse if you disagree.

  • Themes: theme choice stays, as System, Light and Dark. Cyberpunk, Aurora and Velvet Opera are retired, because one product can't have four identities. Anyone using one of them moves to Dark.
  • Fonts: Fraunces, Inter and Space Mono are replaced by Archivo and Spectral italic, loaded from Google Fonts as now.
  • Part colours: Alto moves from sand to rose, and Bass from red to moss. Red now only means a missed note or recording.
  • Tuner lock: locking onto the note fills the target with your colour, instead of turning teal. Teal is now the tenor's colour.
  • Old colour names: the app's current colour variables point at House Lights values from the first day, so screens not yet rebuilt still look like the same product.
  • Brief features that don't exist: Instrument Lab, AI Conductor and analysing an uploaded song are not designed here. They'd be new features, to decide separately.

Build order

In the brief's priority order, one phase at a time. Each phase passes every existing test script and gets your approval before it deploys. Building starts after the noise gate and the warm-up are merged.

  1. Foundation. Fonts, tokens, icons, header and tabs, old colour names remapped, developer text removed, theme choice.
  2. The lighting change. One hook on the shared microphone start and stop, the dimmer, and the singing and results layouts.
  3. Home and the Coach tab.
  4. Vocal Coach tools, one at a time, onto the singing and results layouts.
  5. Choir World: the song page, rehearsal stage, Song Lab, X-Ray, Microscope, mixer.
  6. Performance: Studio as the stage, and the Performance Report.
  7. Progress and identity: You, Journey, Dashboard, Records, both Passports, Voice Health, Profile.
  8. Vocal World games.
  9. Everything else: onboarding, sign-up, Plans, Teacher, Feedback, and the remaining text in four languages.
  10. Final pass: motion, tablet and desktop layouts, accessibility, screenshots of every screen in both themes, and the full test run on production.
